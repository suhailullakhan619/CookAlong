@ rasam | Rasam | Thin, peppery South Indian tamarind and tomato soup, served over rice. | 15 | 40 | 4 | indian,south-indian,soup | Cookbook:Rasam (Tamarind and Tomato Soup) | vegetarian
- 2 tsp oil
- 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- 6 dried red chillies
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 pinch asafoetida
- 14 g toor dal
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 3 tomatoes
- 2 tsp tamarind paste
- 1 tsp sugar or jaggery
- salt
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 4 curry leaves
- 1 handful chopped coriander leaves
> For the rasam powder, toast the coriander seeds, 4 of the chillies, the cumin and the asafoetida in the oil until lightly browned.
> Grind the toasted spices to a powder.
> Wash the toor dal until the water runs clear.
> Cook the dal with double its volume of water and the turmeric for 30 minutes, until mushy.
> Quarter the tomatoes and bring them to the boil in a saucepan of water.
> Add the tamarind, salt and sugar, a little tamarind at a time.
> When the tomatoes are half cooked, add the cooked dal and 4-5 tsp of the rasam powder. Simmer for 5 minutes. {t=300 Simmer}
> Heat the ghee in a small pan. Fry the mustard seeds, urad dal, remaining chillies and curry leaves until the dal browns.
> Pour the seasoning into the rasam and stir it through.
> Add the coriander leaves and serve with hot boiled rice.
