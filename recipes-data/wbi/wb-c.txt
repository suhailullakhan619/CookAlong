- 1 cup oil
- 3 medium onions, thinly sliced
- 8 whole dried red chillies
- 1 kg lean mince (keema), mutton or beef
- 1 tbsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp yogurt
- 2 tbsp raw papaya paste
- 1 charcoal, small piece
- 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
- 1 tsp finely chopped green chillies
> Heat the oil in a heavy saucepan. Fry the onions, stirring often, for 8-10 minutes until light golden.
> Lift out the onions, then fry the whole red chillies in the same oil for just a moment so they don't burn.
> Keep the oil aside. Blend the onions and red chillies to a smooth paste without any water.
> Wash the mince well and squeeze out the water.

> Heat the charcoal over a medium flame until it is red hot and covered in white ash.
> Spread the mince in a metal pan with a well in the centre. Put a piece of foil in the well, set the hot charcoal on it and add 2-3 drops of oil.
> Cover tightly at once and set aside to smoke for 20-25 minutes. {t=1200 Smoke}
> Discard the charcoal. Heat 1/2 cup of the reserved oil in a heavy pan and fry the mince over low heat for 4-5 minutes.
> Cover tightly and cook for 15-20 minutes, until all the moisture is absorbed.
> Serve garnished with the green chillies and coriander leaves.
