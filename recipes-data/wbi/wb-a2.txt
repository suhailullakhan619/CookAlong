- 4 cups sugar
- 2 cups water
- 10 cups milk
- 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
- 4 almonds
- 4 pistachios
> Crumble the paneer, add 2 tbsp of the maida and knead with your palm into a smooth dough.
> Divide into 16 equal portions, roll into balls and press them slightly to flatten.
> Thinly slice the almonds and pistachios.
> Dissolve 2 cups of the sugar in the water and bring to the boil.

> Mix the almonds and pistachios into the milk.
> Gently squeeze the paneer balls and add them to the chilled milk. Refrigerate for 30 more minutes and serve chilled. {t=1800 Chill}
