@ potato-and-eggplant-curry | Potato and Aubergine Curry | Baked aubergine and potato simmered in garam masala, tomato and coconut milk. | 15 | 45 | 2 | indian | potato-and-eggplant-curry | vegetarian
- 1 large aubergine (eggplant)
- 2 medium potatoes
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 onions, finely sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 400 ml canned chopped tomatoes
- 400 ml canned coconut milk
- rice, to serve
> Heat the oven to 200 °C (392 °F).
> Cut the aubergine into 1 cm slices and lay them on a tray. Coat with some of the olive oil and bake for 20 minutes.
> Cook the onions in the rest of the oil over medium heat for 6 minutes, until soft.
> Add the garlic, garam masala, turmeric and ground coriander and stir for 2 minutes, until fragrant.
> Add the chopped tomatoes and coconut milk, then the baked aubergine and the potatoes cut into chunks.
> Cover and simmer for 20 minutes. Meanwhile, boil the rice.
> Uncover and cook for 5 more minutes, until the sauce reduces. Serve with the rice.

@ coriander-chicken | Coriander Chicken | Boneless chicken marinated in a green coriander, yoghurt and chilli paste, cooked with whole spices. | 40 | 20 | 3 | indian | coriander-chicken
- 500 g boneless chicken breast
- 250 g plain yoghurt
- 250 g coriander leaves
- 3 green chillies
- 20 g ginger
- 10 cloves garlic
- 2 medium onions
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- salt
- 3 tbsp oil (mustard or sunflower)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 cardamom pods
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf
> Cut the chicken into small bite-sized pieces.
> Blend the coriander leaves, green chillies, yoghurt, ginger, garlic and onions into a smooth paste.
> Mix half of the paste into the chicken with the garam masala and a little salt.
> Leave the chicken to marinate for 30 minutes.
> Heat the oil in a frying pan. Add the sugar and cook until it melts into the oil.
> Add the cardamom pods, cinnamon stick and bay leaf and cook for 1 minute, then lower the heat to medium.
> Add the marinated chicken and cook, stirring so it doesn't stick, until the oil starts to separate.
> Add the rest of the paste and stir until thick. Season with salt and serve with naan or rice.

@ lamb-biryani | Lamb Biryani | Tamil-style one-pot biryani: lamb simmered with whole spices, then basmati rice cooked in the same pot. | 40 | 75 | 4 | indian,south-indian,rice | lamb-biriyani
- 400 g basmati rice
- 600 g boneless lamb or mutton
- 30 ml neutral oil
- 5 cardamom pods
- 3 bay leaves
- 3 cinnamon sticks
- 8 whole cloves
- 2 red onions
- 2 tomatoes
- 10 cloves garlic
- 1 ginger, 5 cm piece
- 1 green chilli
- 1 pinch turmeric powder
- 1 pinch curry powder
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- salt
- 1/2 tbsp garam masala
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 sprig mint or coriander
> Wash the basmati rice until the water runs clear, then soak it for 30 minutes.
> Dice the tomatoes and slice the onions thinly. Grind the ginger and garlic to a paste. Slit the green chilli lengthways.
> Heat the oil in a big pot over medium heat and toast the cardamom, bay leaves, cinnamon and cloves for 30 seconds.
> Add the onions and sauté for 3 minutes.
> Add the tomatoes and the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 3 more minutes.
> Add the lamb, 500 ml water, the green chilli, turmeric, curry powder, chilli powder and salt. Cook on high heat for 50 minutes, adding water if needed.
> Add enough water to make about 800 ml of liquid in the pot and bring it back to the boil.
> Drain the rice and add it to the pot with the garam masala and lemon juice.
> When most of the water has been absorbed, turn the heat to its lowest and adjust the salt and spice.
> Cover and cook for 10 minutes.
> Garnish with mint or coriander and serve with raita.

@ homemade-yogurt-dahi | Homemade Yogurt (Dahi) | Set your own thick yogurt from milk and a spoonful of starter, kept warm in flasks overnight. | 600 | 10 | 6 | breakfast,healthy | yogurt | vegetarian
- 1 l milk
- 100 ml plain yogurt, as starter
> Heat the milk to 45 °C (113 °F).
> Whisk the yogurt into the milk.
> Bring the milk back to 45 °C. Don't let it get hotter.
> Rinse two 500 ml thermos flasks with about 100 ml of just-boiled water, then pour the water out.
> Pour the milk into the flasks.
> Leave them somewhere without draughts, such as a switched-off oven, for 12 hours.
> Refrigerate the yogurt once it has set.

@ chicken-paprikash | Chicken Paprikash | Hungarian chicken thighs braised in a rich paprika and tomato sauce, finished with sour cream. | 10 | 85 | 8 |  | chicken-paprikash
- 8 chicken thighs (bone-in, skin-on)
- salt
- pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 medium onions, chopped
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/4 cup paprika (Hungarian if possible)
- 2 cups chicken or beef broth
- 1 tbsp cayenne pepper, optional
- 1 cup sour cream, optional
> Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels and season with salt and pepper.
> Heat a little olive oil in a heavy, deep pan over high heat.
> Brown the chicken skin-side down for 5 minutes on each side, then set aside.
> Fry the onions in the chicken fat until golden.
> Add the garlic powder and tomato paste and stir for 5 minutes.
> Add the paprika and stir for 1 more minute.
> Pour in the broth and heat until it is just bubbling.
> Turn the heat to medium-low, return the chicken and its juices to the pan, cover and simmer for 60 minutes.
> Check the chicken is tender, then season with salt and pepper.
> Stir the sour cream into the sauce, dust with cayenne and serve with crusty bread.

@ red-bean-buns-dou-sha-bao | Red Bean Buns (Dou Sha Bao) | Soft, fluffy steamed buns filled with sweet homemade adzuki bean paste. | 120 | 140 | 6 | chinese,snack,breakfast,sweet | dou-sha-bao | vegan
- 250 g adzuki red beans, soaked for 24 hours
- 200 g sugar
- salt
- 350 g wheat flour
- 4 g dried yeast (1 sachet)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 200 ml water
- 1 tsp sunflower oil
> Boil the soaked adzuki beans for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
> Simmer the beans in fresh water for 2 hours, until easily squashed. Drain and blend to a smooth paste.
> Cook the bean paste in a pan over low heat with 150 g of the sugar and a pinch of salt for 10 minutes, stirring, until dry. Cool.
> In a bowl, mix the flour, the rest of the sugar, a pinch of salt, the baking powder and yeast with the water to make a dough.
> Knead the dough for 5 minutes, then add the oil and knead 2 minutes more.
> Cover the dough and leave it to rise for 1 hour.
> Roll the bean paste into 6 balls. Knock the air out of the dough and divide it into 6 pieces.
> Roll each piece into a 10-12 cm disc, put a ball of bean paste in the middle and pleat the edges to seal.
> Set the buns on squares of baking paper in a covered steamer basket and rest for 10 minutes.
> Steam for 20 minutes, then leave covered for 5 minutes before opening.

@ apple-pie | Apple Pie | Double-crust pie with a cinnamon apple filling, reduced on the stove before baking. | 45 | 45 | 8 | sweet,baking,american | apple-pie
- 900 g golden delicious apples
- 15 ml lemon juice
- 50 g white sugar
- 65 g brown sugar
- 5 g cinnamon
- 2 g nutmeg
- 15 g cornstarch
- 350 g flour
- 5 g salt
- 30 g white sugar for the crust
- 225 g cold butter, cut into pieces
- 100 ml ice water
- 1 egg, optional
- 15 ml water, optional
> Peel the apples and cut them into 3-5 cm strips.
> Mix the apples with the lemon juice, 50 g white sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and cornstarch. Leave for 15 minutes to release their juice.

> Line the dish with one circle, trim the excess, then spread in the apple filling.
> Cover with the second circle, tuck in the edges and press the crusts together with a fork.
> Beat the egg with the water and brush it over the top, if using. Cut a few slits in the crust.
> Bake for 45 minutes, until the crust is brown and the filling bubbles.

@ bolo-do-caco | Bolo do Caco | Madeiran sweet potato flatbreads, slow-fermented for 3 days and cooked in a dry pan. | 600 | 45 | 10 | baking | bolo-do-caco
- 750 g sweet potato
- 3 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp active dry yeast
- 150 ml warm water
- 1 kg all-purpose flour
- salted butter, to serve
- parsley, to serve
> Peel the sweet potatoes, cut them into large pieces and cover with water in a saucepan.
> Bring to the boil, cover and cook over medium-high heat for 20 minutes.
> Drain, mash and mix in the salt.
> Mix the yeast with the warm water and leave for 10 minutes.
> Put the flour in a large bowl, make a well and add the mashed sweet potato and yeast.
> Knead into a smooth, elastic dough.
> Cover and leave the dough to ferment in the fridge for 3 days.
> Divide into 10 equal pieces on a floured surface. Shape into balls and flatten to 2.5 cm thick.
> Leave to rest somewhere warm and draught-free for 30 minutes.
> Cook in a dry pan over medium-high heat, turning often, until a thin crust forms on both sides. Roll them on their edges to cook the sides.
> Serve warm with salted butter and parsley.

@ scandinavian-coffee-cake | Scandinavian Coffee Cake | Yeasted cinnamon-sugar ring, sliced and fanned, iced and topped with walnuts. | 180 | 30 | 10 | sweet,baking | scandinavian-coffee-cake
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 7 g active dry yeast (1 packet)
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 3 cups sifted flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- melted butter
- 2/3 cup firmly packed brown sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- powdered sugar icing
- chopped walnuts
> Scald the milk, then stir in the shortening, sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves. Cool to lukewarm.
> Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and add it to the milk mixture.
> Stir in half the flour, add the eggs and beat well. Add enough of the remaining flour to make a soft dough.
> Knead on a lightly floured board until smooth and elastic.
> Put in a greased bowl, brush with shortening, cover and leave somewhere warm for 2 hours, until doubled.

> Sprinkle with the brown sugar and cinnamon, roll up like a Swiss roll and shape into a ring on a greased baking sheet. Pinch the ends together.

> Brush with shortening, cover and leave to rise for 45 minutes, until doubled.
> Heat the oven to 190 °C (375 °F).
> Bake for 30 minutes.
> Spread with the powdered sugar icing while warm and sprinkle with the chopped walnuts.

- 1 cup creamy peanut butter, no sugar added
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 3 tbsp heavy cream or whole milk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1.3 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.8 tsp baking soda
- 0.8 tsp fine sea salt
