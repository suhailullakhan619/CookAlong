@ steamed-egg-custard | Steamed Egg Custard | Silky savoury egg custard steamed until it wobbles like jelly, finished with soy sauce and sesame oil. | 5 | 15 | 1 | chinese,quick,breakfast | vegetable_dish/鸡蛋羹/鸡蛋羹.md
- 2 eggs
- 3 g salt
- 2 ml white vinegar, optional
- 150 ml warm water (about 70 °C)
- 3 ml sesame oil
- 8 ml light soy sauce
> Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the salt and beat well.
> Add the vinegar if using; it takes away the eggy smell.
> Stir in the warm water, 1 to 1.5 times the volume of the egg. Less water gives a firmer custard, more water a softer one.
> Strain the egg mixture to remove the foam, so the custard is smooth. Cover the bowl with a lid, an upturned plate or cling film.
> Bring 300-500 ml of water to a boil in a pot, then put the covered bowl in.
> Steam over medium heat for 10 minutes.
> Turn off the heat and leave the lid on for 5 more minutes.
> Shake the bowl: the custard is done when it is no longer liquid and wobbles like jelly.
> Drizzle with the sesame oil and soy sauce and serve.

- 50 g cornstarch
- 3 g Sichuan pepper salt
- 10 g sesame seeds
- cooking oil, for frying
> Thaw the corn by soaking it in warm water for 15 minutes (or boiling it for 5 minutes). Drain.
> Spread the corn in a sieve or tray lined with paper towels and shake until the paper is soaked.
> Move the corn to a second lined tray and shake again. Repeat until no water drops are visible but the corn is still slightly moist.

- 6 cloves garlic
- 12 ml light soy sauce
- 1 handful sesame seeds
- 10 ml Chinese black vinegar
- 12 ml cooking oil
> Cut the onion into small pieces and slice the ginger. Spread them over the base of a wide pan.
> Rinse the prawns (deveining and trimming the legs is optional), drain and lay them on the onion and ginger.
> Pour in the rice wine, cover and cook over medium heat for 1 minute.
> Turn the heat to low and cook for 5 minutes.
> Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes more.
> Meanwhile, make the dip: chop the scallion and mince the garlic, then mix them with the soy sauce, sesame seeds and vinegar.
> Heat the oil until hot and pour it over the dip.
> Lift the prawns onto a clean plate and serve with the dip.

@ steamed-pork-patty-egg | Steamed Pork Patty with Egg | Seasoned minced pork steamed on a plate with a whole egg in the centre. | 10 | 20 | 2 | chinese | meat_dish/肉饼炖蛋.md
- 300 g minced pork
- 2 eggs
- 10 ml Shaoxing rice wine
- 20 ml light soy sauce
- 5 g white pepper powder
- 12 ml sesame oil
> In a bowl, mix the pork with the rice wine, soy sauce, white pepper, 1 of the eggs and the sesame oil until well combined.
> Spread the pork over a plate. Make a hollow in the middle with a spoon and crack the other egg into it.
> Fill a pot a quarter full with water and bring to a boil. Put in the plate, cover and steam for 15 minutes.
