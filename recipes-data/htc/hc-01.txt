- 2 tomatoes (about 360 g)
- 3 eggs
- 12 ml cooking oil
- 3 g salt
- 2 g sugar, optional
- 1 tbsp chopped scallion, optional
> Wash the tomatoes. Optionally blanch them in boiling water, then cold water, and peel. Cut the tomatoes into pieces no bigger than 4 cm.
> Crack the eggs into a bowl, add 2 g of the salt and beat well.
> Heat the wok and add the oil. When it is hot, pour in the eggs and stir-fry until just set and lightly yellow. Turn off the heat and move the eggs to a plate. Do not wash the wok.
> Turn the heat back on and add the tomato pieces. Press them with the spatula and stir-fry for 20 seconds, or until soft.
> Return the eggs to the wok and toss everything together.
> Add the remaining salt, the sugar if you like it sweeter, and the scallion. Stir to mix, then turn off the heat and serve.

- 300 g cauliflower
- 3 cloves garlic
- 3 g salt
- 15 ml cooking oil
- 50 ml water
> Wash the cauliflower and break it into small florets. Slice any thick stems. Peel the garlic and slice it.
> Bring 1000 ml of water to a boil. Blanch the cauliflower for 2-3 minutes, until it turns paler and slightly soft. Drain.
> Heat the oil in a wok over high heat. Add the garlic slices and fry briefly until fragrant.
> Add the cauliflower and stir-fry over medium-high heat for about 2 minutes.
> Add the salt and keep stirring.
> Pour the water down the side of the wok, cover and steam for 1 minute so the cauliflower cooks through.
> Uncover, toss quickly and serve.

- 1 cauliflower (about 300 g)
- 1 onion (about 100 g)
- 1 tomato (about 100 g)
- 8 g fresh ginger
- 3 cloves garlic (about 9 g)
- 1 green chilli
- 30 ml cooking oil
- 3 g cumin seeds
- 3 g turmeric powder
- 3 g red chilli powder
- 5 g ground coriander
- 3 g garam masala
- 5 g salt
- 15 g fresh coriander leaves

- 8 g cornstarch
- 150 ml cooking oil
- 5 g scallion
- 15 g garlic, minced
- 5 g ginger, minced
- 15 ml doubanjiang (chilli bean paste)
- 10 ml light soy sauce
- 10 g sugar
- 80 ml water
