@ blueberry-pancakes | Blueberry Pancakes | Fluffy American breakfast pancakes spiced with cinnamon and nutmeg, with blueberries folded into the batter. | 15 | 20 | 4 | american,breakfast | Cookbook:Blueberry Pancakes
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 tsp cream of tartar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 4 eggs, separated
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup frozen blueberries
> Sift together the flour, sugar, baking powder, cream of tartar, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg. Set aside.
> Whisk the buttermilk, melted butter, vanilla and egg yolks in a large bowl. Mix in the flour mixture, then the blueberries.

- 130 g whole wheat flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 egg
- 250 ml milk
- 2 tbsp oil
- oil, for frying
> Mix the whole wheat flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
> In another bowl, beat the egg, then stir in the milk and the 2 tbsp oil.
