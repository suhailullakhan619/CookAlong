@ scones | Scones | Light, golden British tea scones, served split with jam and cream. | 15 | 11 | 10 | british,baking,sweet | Cookbook:Plain Scones
- 450 g plain flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 3 tbsp caster sugar
- 5 tbsp cold butter, cut into pieces
- 1 egg
- 150 ml heavy cream, plus extra for brushing
- fruit jam, to serve
> Preheat the oven to 230 °C (450 °F).
> Pulse the plain flour, salt, baking powder and 2 tbsp of the caster sugar in a food processor. Add the butter and pulse to a cornmeal texture.
> Add the egg and just enough heavy cream to make a slightly sticky dough.
> Turn the dough onto a floured surface, knead once or twice and press into a 2 cm thick circle. Cut out 5 cm rounds.
> Place the rounds on an ungreased baking sheet and reshape the leftover dough to cut more scones.
> Brush the tops with a little cream and sprinkle with the remaining sugar.
> Bake for 10 minutes, until golden brown. {t=600 Bake}
> Cool slightly and serve with fruit jam and extra cream.
